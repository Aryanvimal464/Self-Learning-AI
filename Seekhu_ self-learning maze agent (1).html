How it learns

This is Q-learning, a form of reinforcement learning. The agent keeps a table with one score for every cell and every move (up, right, down, left). The score answers one question: how good is this move from this cell in the long run?

It begins by moving at random. Reaching the goal gives +1, falling in a pit gives -1, and every step costs a tiny bit so it prefers short routes. After each move it nudges the score of that move towards what it just saw plus the best score available from the next cell:

Q(s,a) ← Q(s,a) + α · ( reward + γ · max Q(s′,·) − Q(s,a) )

Good news travels backwards from the goal one cell at a time, which is why the green glow spreads from the goal towards the start. Early on it explores at random. As curiosity fades it follows its own arrows more and more. Try the cliff maze: with a high farsightedness it finds the safe route, and it is slower but never falls.

Edit the maze while it is learning, or change the sliders, to see what each setting does.
